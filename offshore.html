Keelung Port Offshore Shore Excursion Programs

Trip 1 · Iconic Highlights

Taipei 101, National Revolutionary Martyrs' Shrine & National Palace Museum

Taipei 101 · Martyrs' Shrine · National Palace Museum
  • National Palace Museum: Marvel at one of the world's greatest collections of nearly 700,000 ancient Chinese imperial art treasures and jade artifacts spanning over 5,000 years.
  • National Revolutionary Martyrs' Shrine: Witness the world-renowned, precisely synchronized changing of the military honor guard ceremony.
  • Taipei 101 Observatory: Ascend via one of the world's fastest elevators to the 89th-floor observatory for breathtaking 360-degree panoramic views across the entire Taipei metropolitan basin.
Trip 2 · Cultural Heritage & Old Town

Historic Dadaocheng, Traditional Temples & The Grand Hotel

Baoan Temple · Confucius Temple · Dihua Street · Yongle Market · Xiahai City God Temple · The Grand Hotel
  • Baoan Temple & Confucius Temple: Explore UNESCO Asia-Pacific Cultural Heritage Award-winning temple architecture and intricate folk artisan wood and stone carvings.
  • Dihua Street & Dadaocheng: Stroll along Taipei's oldest commercial quarter, famous for traditional tea merchant houses, herbal medicine dispensaries, and the bustling Yongle fabric market.
  • The Grand Hotel Taipei: Stop for photographs at the majestic palace-style historic hotel once built to host international heads of state.
Trip 3 · Modern Marvel & Sacred Roots

Taipei 101 Observatory & Historic Longshan Temple

Taipei 101 Skyscraper · Historical Longshan Temple in Bangka
  • Longshan Temple (Bangka): Founded in 1738, experience Taipei's most revered temple steeped in vibrant incense rituals, Buddhist and Taoist harmony.
  • Taipei 101 Modern District: Contrast Taipei's centuries-old heritage with the sleek high-end shopping and architectural wonders of the Xinyi district.
Trip 4 · Mountain Village Romance

Jiufen Nostalgic Gold Mining Village & Teahouse Tour

Chiufen (Jiufen mountain village, traditional teahouses & coastal cliffs)
  • Jiufen Old Street: Wander through stone-paved hillside stairways lined with red lanterns, handicraft shops, and the famous A-Mei Teahouse overlooking the Pacific Ocean.
  • Local Delicacies: Savor authentic freshly made handmade taro balls, herbal rice cakes, and world-famous high-mountain Oolong tea.
Trip 5 · World Geological Wonders

Yehliu Geopark & Coastal Rock Formations

Yehliu Geo Park (Queen's Head, Fairy Shoe & marine sandstone hoodoos)
  • Yehliu Geopark: Discover nature's geological marvels sculpted over millennia by sea waves and winds, including the world-famous "Queen's Head" rock.
  • Scenic Northern Coast: Take in stunning coastal sea cliffs, ocean breezes, and natural open-air geology exhibits along Taiwan's North Coast.
Custom Port Excursions: We also provide customized shore excursion itineraries for cruise passengers docking at Kaohsiung Port Cruise Terminal (Lotus Pond Dragon & Tiger Pagodas, Pier-2 Art Center, British Consulate at Takow, and Fo Guang Shan). Contact us directly for a tailored quote.
